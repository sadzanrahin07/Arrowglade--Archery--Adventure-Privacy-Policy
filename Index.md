## Local Gameplay Data

To provide a seamless experience and save your progress offline, the Game stores gameplay information locally on your device. This includes:

* Chapter and stage progress, performance stars, scores, and achievements.
* Virtual coins, training experience, equipment levels, selected powers, unlocked characters, and skins.
* Sound, sensitivity, accessibility preferences, and other game settings.
* Local records used to apply game rewards and upgrades accurately.

We do not operate a cloud-save server or player account system. Your device or operating system may back up local app data depending on your device settings; those backups are managed by your device or backup provider rather than by us.

* Your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address and approximate location inferred from it.

* Ad impressions, ad interactions (such as views and clicks), and configuration of the app when utilizing our Service.
* Diagnostic information, including crash logs, performance data, and the time and date of your use of the Service.

We configure our advertising settings to serve **non-personalized, family-safe advertisements**. We do not use interest-based advertising, remarketing, or cross-app behavioral profiling, and we do not share your local gameplay profile with advertising networks.
